Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026 (version 2.0)

Overview

Mindful Prism does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. This policy explains how the app handles your information on your device.

Data collected

None. Mindful Prism collects no personal information, usage data, analytics, crash reports, or any other data from users. The developer never receives your journal entries, recordings, or health data.

How your journal entries are processed

All analysis is performed entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework (Apple Intelligence). Your journal entries are never sent to any server, third party, or cloud service. Entries exist only in your device's memory during your session and are cleared when you close the app or start over.

Ways you can add an entry

  • Voice (microphone and speech recognition). If you use live transcription, the app asks for microphone and speech recognition access. Your speech is transcribed on your device. Audio is not recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.
  • Camera (scanning handwritten pages). If you scan a page, the camera image is used only to recognize the text on your device. The image is not saved by the app.
  • PDF import. If you import a PDF, the app reads its text on your device. The app only sees files you choose.

You can turn off any of these permissions at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

Mindful Prism uses HealthKit only to save your guided breathing sessions as Mindful Minutes, and only when you tap Save to Apple Health. The app writes this data; it does not read any data from Apple Health.

Mindful Minutes are stored in Apple's Health app under your control, not by Mindful Prism. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone. You can manage or remove this access at any time in the Health app or in Settings.

Prism Prints and keepsakes

Mindful Prism can create a Prism Print image of your entry and a monthly painting. These are kept only if you choose to keep them, and they stay on your device. If you share a Prism Print or save it to Photos, it goes only where you send it, using the standard iOS share sheet. Mindful Prism does not upload it anywhere.

Let it go

Words you write in the "let something go" step during breathing are never saved. They are discarded as soon as the animation ends.

Face ID and biometric data

If you enable the Face ID lock, authentication is handled entirely by iOS and Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. Mindful Prism never sees, stores, or accesses your biometric data.

Third-party services

None. Mindful Prism uses no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or external APIs of any kind.

Children's privacy

Mindful Prism does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy
