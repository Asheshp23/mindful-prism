Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

Mindful Prism does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. This policy explains exactly how the app handles your information.

Data collected

None. Mindful Prism collects no personal information, usage data, analytics, crash reports, or any other data from users.

How your journal entries are processed

All analysis is performed entirely on your device using Apple's on-device AI framework (FoundationModels). Your journal entries are never sent to any server, third party, or cloud service. They exist only in your device's memory during your session and are not written to disk by the app.

Face ID and biometric data

If you enable the Face ID lock, authentication is handled entirely by iOS and Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. Mindful Prism never sees, stores, or accesses your biometric data.

Third-party services

None. Mindful Prism uses no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or external APIs of any kind.

Children's privacy

Mindful Prism does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new last updated date.
