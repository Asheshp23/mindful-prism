Mindful Prism

About

Mindful Prism uses on-device AI to turn your journal entries into deep, meaningful insights — privately, instantly, and entirely on your iPhone. No account. No cloud. No data collection.

What you get

  • Emotional analysis — feelings, triggers, and intensity
  • CBT mind patterns — thinking traps and healthier perspectives
  • Sentiment analysis with confidence and reasoning
  • Value analysis — what matters to you and what's being tested
  • Personality insights based on the Big Five model
  • Narrative analysis — your role in your own story
  • Reflection prompts to take your thinking further

Privacy

Everything runs on your device using Apple's on-device AI framework. Your journal entries never leave your iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers, and no data collected of any kind.
